# Worked product-image patterns

## Clothing + model

Reference 1 supplies the person and base photograph; reference 2 supplies the garment. Describe the garment's visible neckline, belt, straps and hem, then preserve the person's face, stance and lighting. Example instruction: “Replace the outfit with the garment in reference 2; let its fabric and shadows follow the original pose. Preserve the face, hair, framing and shoes. No additional jewelry or text.” Check fit, occlusion, face identity and changed garment details at full size.

## Product + background

Reference 1 supplies the product, reference 2 the environment. Example instruction: “Use reference 1 only for the product. Use reference 2 for the bench, wall and left window light. Preserve the handle, zippers and visible strap; add a plausible contact shadow. Do not copy the original white background.” Check hardware count, perspective, product color and contact.

## Remove a prop

Specify the exact prop and its shadow. Example instruction: “Remove only the small orange cube on the right and its shadow. Reconstruct the warm ivory tabletop. Preserve the headphones, headband stitching, earcup texture, camera angle, crop and existing product shadow.” Inspect the inpainted boundary and product edges.

Full observed workspace cases and images: [FusionAI examples](https://github.com/cjbjjob-sketch/fusionai-skills/tree/main/examples). These prompts are reusable patterns, not a guarantee that generation will preserve every detail.
